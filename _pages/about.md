Rasyad A. Parinduri is an Associate Professor of Economics at the University of Nottingham Malaysia. He does research on the intersection of development economics, labor economics, and industrial
organization using applied microeconometrics, analyzing the effects of policies and market changes
on development, competition, labor outcomes, and trade. He has published in, among others, the
Journal of Development Studies, Economics of Transition, Economics of Education Review, Oxford
Economic Papers, and World Development.
